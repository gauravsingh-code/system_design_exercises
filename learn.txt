&  - means we are passing reference , no copy and no ownership
   - alias to same object
   - can not be a nullptr

* - we are passing the address of that object
  - stores the address that persistes beyond the functi call
  - ownership &  storage
  - can be nullptr

Simple Rule of Thumb :- 
Use & when you just look at something.
Use * when you need to store or own something.

If NO (just printing/reading) ➔ Use const auto &v.


-------Factory Method
1. "I have if-else creation repeated in 10 different files."	➔ 
    Use Simple Factory to centralize it into 1 file.
2. "I want other developers to add new products without editing my factory."	➔ 
    Use Factory Method so subclasses override creation.
3. "I have related products (Theme, Ecosystem) that must match and never be mixed."	➔ 
    Use Abstract Factory to create the whole suite together.


Level 1: Simple Factory     ──► One centralized class with a switch/case
Level 2: Factory Method     ──► Polymorphic factories (subclasses decide what to create)
Level 3: Abstract Factory   ──► Creates FAMILIES of related products together


--- FACADE Pattern --
The Facade Pattern provides a single, 
simple front-door interface to hide the complexity of an entire subsystem.
